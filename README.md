### Hi, I'm Antonio 👋

I build **automations and small internal tools for trade businesses**: window & door installers, solar installers, home-improvement companies.

Before writing software I installed windows and doors on real job sites. I've seen how leads get lost: a missed call, a form nobody answered, a quote nobody chased. That's the problem I solve now.

**What I build**

- **Lead follow-up systems**: every new lead (website form, trade-show stand, voice note from the field) is saved, the team is alerted on Telegram with action buttons, and automatic follow-ups go out until someone calls back.
- **Site-survey and quoting tools**: measurements entered on a tablet during the visit, quote ready before you leave the customer's house.
- **Custom n8n workflows**: webhooks, APIs, AI steps (classification, reply reading, data extraction), error handling and monitoring.
